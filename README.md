# Tysk Ordforråd

Øv tysk ordforråd med tale – inkl. 6 eksamensmoduler.

## Brug på telefon (anbefalet)

**Live app:** [https://alexzille.github.io/german-vocab/](https://alexzille.github.io/german-vocab/)

1. Åbn linket i **Chrome** (Android) eller **Safari** (iPhone)
2. Tillad mikrofon
3. **Installer som app:** iPhone → Del → Føj til hjemmeskærm · Android → menu → Installer app
4. Vælg eksamensmodul under Practice og start øvelsen

Virker overalt med internet – computeren skal ikke være tændt.

## Features

- **Audio-Driven Practice**: Hear German words and respond with Danish translations using your voice
- **Speech Recognition**: Uses Web Speech API to recognize your Danish answers
- **Text-to-Speech**: Pronounces German words clearly
- **Word Mastery Tracking**: Tracks which words you've mastered
- **Spaced Repetition**: Automatically schedules reviews to prevent forgetting
- **Statistics Dashboard**: View your progress, streaks, and mastered words
- **Neutral Design**: Clean, minimal interface with neutral colors

## How to Use

### Getting Started

1. **Open the App**: Open `index.html` in a modern web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended for best speech recognition support)

2. **Allow Microphone Access**: When prompted, allow the browser to access your microphone

3. **Start Practice**: Click "Start Practice" to begin a session

### Practice Flow

1. The app will speak a German word
2. You respond by speaking the Danish translation
3. The app will tell you if you're correct or incorrect
4. After answering, it automatically moves to the next word

### Controls

- **Start Practice**: Begin a new practice session
- **Repeat Word**: Hear the current German word again
- **Stop**: End the current practice session

### Navigation

- **Practice**: Main practice screen
- **Statistics**: View your progress, mastered words, and statistics
- **Settings**: Adjust mastery threshold, TTS speed, and words per session

## Settings

- **Mastery Threshold**: Number of correct answers needed to master a word (default: 3)
- **TTS Speed**: Speed of German word pronunciation (0.5x - 2.0x)
- **Words per Session**: Number of words to practice in each session (5-50)

## Statistics

The statistics screen shows:
- Total words mastered
- Mastery percentage
- Day streak
- Words learned today
- Category breakdown
- List of all mastered words

## Technical Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox)

- **Chrome/Edge**: Full support (recommended)
- **Firefox**: May require additional setup

## Data Storage

All progress is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Your data stays on your device and is not sent to any server.

## Vocabulary

The app includes 20 beginner German words across categories:
- Common words (Haus, Wasser, Buch, etc.)
- Animals (Hund, Katze)
- Food (Apfel, Brot, Milch)
- Nature (Baum, Sonne, Garten)
- Family (Mutter, Vater)
- And more...

You can expand the vocabulary by editing `vocabulary.json`.

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Practice Daily**: Even 10-15 minutes daily is better than long sessions occasionally
2. **Quiet Environment**: Use the app in a quiet place for better speech recognition
3. **Speak Clearly**: Enunciate your Danish translations clearly
4. **Review Regularly**: The spaced repetition system will bring back words for review
5. **Check Statistics**: Monitor your progress to stay motivated
